Open lines

Vendor and amount start empty. Type a name and a figure straight into the boxes on each line. They are ordinary text fields, and the totals above change as you type. You do not need to edit this page’s code. The photos are mood references for the florals, the lights, and the welcome sign: a soft romantic garden in dusty blush, ivory, and eucalyptus.

Open 1

Ceremony florals

Dusty blush, ivory, and cream, with eucalyptus. A wood or slim metal arch with floor-length blush and ivory sheers. Petal scatter down the aisle, and meadow borders along both sides, together with the flowers on the arch. Garden roses, white hydrangea, and baby’s breath — a soft romantic Western garden. Please keep the lawn aisle to that same flower list.

Open 2

Dinner / long-table florals

One long banquet table for dinner, in rustic wood or white linen, in place of rounds. A low runner of blush and cream roses, baby’s breath, and greenery, with taper candles. An optional circular greenery “moon” arch may sit over the table.

Open 3

Fairy / bistro lights

A warm globe or bistro canopy over the lawn. Vertical strands on the arch are optional. Hurricane lanterns along the aisle are welcome.

Open 4

Welcome sign / stationery décor

A fabric or framed welcome, with white florals and a greenery garland. A burgundy accent is welcome. Please letter the sign for Kam and Stephanie. The names on the sample photo are a layout reference only.

Open 5

Live music + ceremony/dinner PA

A jazz trio, or something close to it, playing through about 22:00, with PA for the ceremony and for dinner. Please propose the vendor and the fee.

Open 6

Planning / day-of coordination

Open 7

Other / contingency

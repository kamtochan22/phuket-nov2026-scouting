Other lines, if useful

Open 3

Ceremony florals

Option A · primary. Dusty blush, ivory, and cream, with eucalyptus. A wood or slim metal arch with floor-length blush and ivory sheers. Petal scatter down the aisle, and meadow borders along both sides, together with the flowers on the arch. Garden roses, white hydrangea, and baby’s breath — a soft romantic Western garden. Please keep the lawn aisle to that same flower list.

Option B · alternative look. A white petal-scatter aisle on the lawn, with light wood cane or rattan Louis-style chairs and white seats. Two free-standing floral pillars, set asymmetrically, in white and cream with terracotta and burnt-orange accents and dried foliage, instead of a full arch with sheers. A gold-framed welcome sign, with flowers at the base. The names on that sample sign are a layout reference only — this couple is Kam and Stephanie. The ocean in the photo is the sample’s setting only; the ceremony is still the Green Lawn. Both options are open. Picking one later may make the other a poor fit, but for now an honest read on either direction is enough.

One name and one figure below is enough, for whichever direction feels closer.

Open 4

Dinner / long-table florals

One long banquet table for dinner, in rustic wood or white linen, in place of rounds. A low runner of blush and cream roses, baby’s breath, and greenery, with taper candles. An optional circular greenery “moon” arch may sit over the table.

Open 5

Fairy / bistro lights

A warm globe or bistro canopy over the lawn. Vertical strands on the arch are optional. Hurricane lanterns along the aisle are welcome.

Open 6

Welcome sign / stationery décor

A fabric or framed welcome, with white florals and a greenery garland. A burgundy accent is welcome. Please letter the sign for Kam and Stephanie. The names on the sample photo are a layout reference only.
